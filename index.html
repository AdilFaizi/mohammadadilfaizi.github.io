01 — About Me

My Story

I am Adil Faizi, an Electrical Engineering student at the Lassonde School of Engineering, York University, with an expected graduation in 2028. My academic and professional interests are centred on power systems, electrical infrastructure, and energy distributionfields that sit at the intersection of technical complexity and real-world societal impact.

I chose Electrical Engineering because I am drawn to solving technical problems and understanding how large-scale systems operate. The electrical grid is one of the most critical infrastructures in modern society, and I am particularly fascinated by how power is generated, transmitted, and distributed reliably and efficiently. Currently, I am gaining hands-on experience as a Distribution Investment Planning Co-op Student at Hydro One (May 2026 – August 2027), where I work with complex power systems documents and contribute to planning decisions that affect real communities.

Beyond technical skills, I am committed to growing as a communicator, collaborator, and lifelong learner. My long-term goal is to obtain my Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) designation in Ontario, pursue graduate studies in Electrical Engineering with a focus on power, and potentially contribute to research in Hybrid Energy Storage Systems (HESS).
