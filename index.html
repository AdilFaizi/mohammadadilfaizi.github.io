01 — About Me

My Story

I am Adil Faizi, an Electrical Engineering student at the Lassonde School of Engineering, York University, with an expected graduation in 2028. My academic and professional interests are centred on power systems, electrical infrastructure, and energy distribution — fields that sit at the intersection of technical complexity and real-world societal impact.

I chose Electrical Engineering because I am drawn to solving technical problems and understanding how large-scale systems operate. The electrical grid is one of the most critical infrastructures in modern society, and I am particularly fascinated by how power is generated, transmitted, and distributed reliably and efficiently. Currently, I am gaining hands-on experience as a Distribution Investment Planning Co-op Student at Hydro One (May 2026 – August 2027), where I work with complex power systems documents and contribute to planning decisions that affect real communities.

Beyond technical skills, I am committed to growing as a communicator, collaborator, and lifelong learner. My long-term goal is to obtain my Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) designation in Ontario, pursue graduate studies in Electrical Engineering with a focus on power, and potentially contribute to research in Hybrid Energy Storage Systems (HESS).

03 — Discover Myself · Dreams & Goals

Dreams & Goals

My SMART Goals are organized around three core competencies identified through self-assessment. Each goal is tied to my co-op experience at Hydro One and my broader professional development as an engineering student.

Competency 1 — Accessing and Analyzing Information

Through my SWOT Analysis self-assessment, I identified a significant gap in my ability to evaluate and extract information effectively. In my co-op placement, I encounter complex power systems documents that require both theoretical and technical understanding — and errors have real consequences. Beyond the workplace, strong information literacy is a skill I want to prioritize as a developing professional engineer and lifelong learner.

SMART 01

Credibility & Attentive Reading

Improve my ability to distinguish credible information from misinformation by practising attentive reading on three technical documents per week during this co-op, tracking comprehension through written summaries after each document.

🗓 Target: December 2026
Information Literacy
SMART 02

Extracting Key Information

Develop the ability to extract key information from complex documents by applying structured note-taking to all assigned co-op documents and reviewing those notes weekly to reinforce retention.

🗓 Target: December 2026
Technical Comprehension

Competency 2 — Communication Skills

My SWOT Analysis included communication as a weakness, and entering a professional environment reinforced this. I have noticed how relying on informal language limits how I structure my thoughts. These goals build directly on Competency 1 — once I can access and analyze information, the next step is to communicate it professionally and clearly.

SMART 03

Oral Communication

Improve oral communication by actively reviewing how I respond to technical and non-technical questions at work each week, identifying at least one area for improvement, and adjusting my language and structure in subsequent conversations.

🗓 Target: December 2026
Professional Communication
SMART 04

Vocabulary & Technical Terminology

Expand my vocabulary by reading an industry-relevant article or engineering publication (e.g., IEEE) bi-weekly and incorporating new terminology into my spoken and written communication.

🗓 Target: December 2026
Technical Writing

Competency 3 — Collaboration & Leadership

As an engineering student, I know that technical skills alone are not sufficient. Being able to work with others and take initiative creates opportunities and relationships that cannot be found any other way. My co-op provides an environment where I can explore many areas of the power industry and build real connections with professionals.

SMART 05

Strengthening Collaboration

Strengthen collaboration skills by speaking to my planners about plans of action for specific tasks and understanding the thought process behind certain steps, building my ability to work effectively within a professional team.

🗓 Target: May 2027
Teamwork
SMART 06

Taking Initiative

Take initiative by proposing improvement processes or solutions to workplace inefficiencies encountered during my co-op term, documenting them, and sharing them with my senior planner — reflecting my growth as a proactive co-op student.

🗓 Target: May 2027
Leadership

Long-Term Career Goals

  • → Obtain P.Eng. — Professional Engineer designation (Ontario)
  • → Continue to Graduate Studies — MEng or MSc in Electrical Engineering (Power focus)
  • → Research Hybrid Energy Storage Systems (HESS) during undergraduate studies
  • → Potentially pursue an MBA degree to combine engineering expertise with business leadership
