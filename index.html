01 — About

My Story

Welcome to my ePortfolio — a living document of my journey as a student and growing professional. Portfolios are more than collections of work; they are stories of knowing: knowing about things, knowing oneself, and knowing your audience.

Here, I've carefully selected pieces of my best work that demonstrate the quality and variety of my academic activity developed over time. I've also included works-in-progress to reflect honestly on where I'm headed and what I'm still learning.

This portfolio connects my academic experiences to real-world applications, helping me articulate my skills and strengths to future employers, mentors, and collaborators.
