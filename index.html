My Story

I am an Electrical Engineering student at York University with a strong interest in power systems, electrical infrastructure, and energy distribution. My academic experiences and co-op opportunities have helped me develop both technical and professional skills while exploring how engineering solutions impact communities and society. I chose Electrical Engineering because I enjoy solving technical problems and understanding how complex systems operate. The electrical grid is one of the most important infrastructures in modern society, and I am particularly interested in how power is generated, transmitted, and distributed reliably and efficiently.
